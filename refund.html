Refund Policy

Effective September 19, 2026. This policy explains when money is and is not refundable in connection with KR Management Team.

1. Inquiries are free

Contact, Meet & Greet, VIP, newsletter, and support forms on this hub do not require payment. A KRM reference ID is not a receipt for money.

2. No payment through unofficial messages

Do not send money to a personal account, gift-card code, cryptocurrency wallet, or cash-app name because a social-media profile used the KR Management name. Those transfers are not sales through this hub and cannot be refunded by this team.

3. Documented offerings only

A Shop listing, including the KR Fan Card, is an information card until the team confirms the order in writing. Confirmation must state the item, price, what is included, shipping or pickup method, and the official payment destination.

4. If a confirmed purchase is cancelled by the team

If KR Management Team cancels a confirmed offering before delivery, the amount paid to the official destination named in the written confirmation is refundable to the original payment method where the processor allows it.

5. If you cancel a confirmed purchase

If you cancel after written confirmation and before shipment, a refund may be issued minus documented processing or production costs stated in the confirmation. After shipment, return terms in the confirmation control the result.

6. Experiences and dates

Meet-and-greet or VIP experiences, when later offered in writing, are date-specific. A missed date caused by the visitor is not an automatic refund. A cancelled or rescheduled date caused by the team is handled in the written confirmation for that experience.

7. How to request a refund

Email krmanagementteamsent@gmail.com with the subject Refund Request. Include the KRM reference ID, the date of the written confirmation, the amount, and the official destination you paid. Allow a reasonable review period after the request arrives.

8. Chargebacks

Contact the team first when a confirmed payment needs review. A chargeback filed against an authorized, delivered item may be contested with the confirmation record.
