Effective September 19, 2026. KR Management merchandise is paid on Stripe. Refunds, when due, are returned to the original Stripe payment method.

Unless a product page says otherwise: unopened merchandise may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund of the merchandise price, excluding shipping. Opened, engraved, or worn items are not returnable.
