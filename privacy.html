Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026. KR Management Team operates this hub for fan relations, merchandise, and reviewed experiences supporting Kid Rock.

Information we collect

Inquiry, newsletter, and Stripe checkout collect only what you provide: name, email, phone, country, shipping address, message content, and payment details handled by Stripe. We do not see full card numbers. Stripe processes cards on our behalf.

How we use it

To review requests, fulfill merchandise, send a receipt, reply through the method you listed, and keep a record of written confirmations. Inquiry data is emailed to the KR Management intake desk, including GoDaddy Conversations.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We never ask for passwords, authentication codes, government identification, or banking logins. If a message requests those, it is not this desk.

Retention and contact

We keep inquiry and order records as needed to fulfill, refund, and prevent fraud. To update or delete a newsletter listing, write krmanagementteamsent@gmail.com.
