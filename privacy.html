Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026. This policy describes how KR Management Team handles information submitted through the official hub at team.kidrockmanagementteamsent.com and hub.kidrockmanagementteamsent.com.

1. Who we are

KR Management Team operates this hub as a professional management and fan-relations destination. Questions about this policy can be sent to krmanagementteamsent@gmail.com with the subject Privacy Request.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send through a published form or email: full name, email address, phone number, country, inquiry type, preferred date, guest count, message, and consent confirmation. The hub also creates a KRM reference ID for each accepted request. Server logs may include the time of submission and a truncated network address used only for abuse control.

3. Information we do not collect

This hub does not ask for passwords, authentication codes, government identification, Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, card CVV codes, seed phrases, or cryptocurrency wallet keys. Any page that asks for those items is not this hub.

4. How information is used

Inquiry details are used to review the request, issue a reference ID, and reply through the contact method you provided. Newsletter details, if you opt in, are used only for approved KR Management updates. We do not sell visitor lists.

5. Where information is delivered

Form submissions are processed on the hub server and routed to the team’s GoDaddy Conversations inbox. A copy may also be delivered to krmanagementteamsent@gmail.com. Delivery providers see the fields required to send the message.

6. Retention

Inquiry records are kept as long as needed to review the request, prevent abuse, and maintain a record of the reference ID. You may ask for a copy or deletion of your inquiry by emailing krmanagementteamsent@gmail.com and including the KRM reference ID if you have one.

7. Cookies and analytics

The hub is a static public site. It does not run advertising pixels and does not build a marketing profile from your browsing. Hosting infrastructure may store standard technical logs.

8. Children

This hub is intended for adults making management or fan inquiries. Do not submit information about a child without the appropriate adult authorization.

9. Your choices

You may decline to submit a form. You may unsubscribe from optional updates by emailing krmanagementteamsent@gmail.com with the subject Unsubscribe. You may request correction of an inquiry record through the same address.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
